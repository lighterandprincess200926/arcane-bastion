Privacy Policy — Arcane Bastion

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Arcane Bastion ("the game") is developed by Duc Nguyen. This policy explains what information is collected when you play the game and how it is used.

Information we collect

The game itself does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or any account information. There is no sign-in. Your game progress (level, gear, skills and settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Ad interactions (which ads you saw or tapped) and app usage events
  • Diagnostic and performance data

This information is collected and used by Google under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: On first launch, iOS asks whether the game may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): Where required by law, the game shows Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app. We do not keep any copy of it. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy policy linked above.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available on this page.
