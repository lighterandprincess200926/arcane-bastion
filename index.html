Arcane Bastion

A wizard tower defense RPG for iPhone. Master Water, Earth, Fire and Lightning and defend the last castle across 100 maps.

FAQ

Is my progress saved? Yes, automatically on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Why do I see ads? Ads keep the game free. One ad is shown after each map.

How do I stop personalized ads? Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off tracking for Arcane Bastion.
